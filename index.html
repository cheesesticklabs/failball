Lesson vocabulary

Language model
An AI trained to find patterns in language and use them to generate text.
Prompt
The instructions or question you give an AI.
Hallucination
When an AI gives made-up or unsupported information as if it were true.
Source
Where information came from, such as a study, book, or reliable website.
Confidence
How sure an answer sounds. Confident wording does not prove an answer is correct.
Random
An outcome that is uncertain. Random does not automatically mean every possible result has an equal chance.
Prime number
A whole number greater than 1 that is divisible evenly only by 1 and itself.
Pattern
A repeated feature or regularity in information.
